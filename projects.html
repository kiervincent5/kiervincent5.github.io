Capstone Project

Plastic waste disposal is one of the most significant environmental concerns today that jeopardize not only the health of our planet, but also the existence of human beings. More than 19 to 23 million metric tons of plastic enter aquatic ecosystems annually and an unbelievable 2,000 garbage trucks of plastic are dumped into oceans, rivers, and lakes on a daily basis. Our solution to aid in the healing of Mother Earth is the EcoDrop machine, which promotes recycling by using a reward system.
